      My hikes

        Get ready to hike

        This app shows where you are on a map, even with no cell signal. Set it up at home, on Wi-Fi:

          A phone is a helper, not a plan. This app only records and tracks you while it's open on screen, so don't lock your phone or switch apps for long while recording. Tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back. Bring water, warm layers, a headlamp and a power bank. Turn back if the weather, the trail or the daylight isn't on your side. In an emergency, call 911.

          Before you start

          Keep this app open on screen while you hike. If you lock your phone or switch to another app, your phone stops sharing your location with it. That part of your hike won't be recorded, and the map won't know where you are.
          • The screen stays on by itself while recording, so you don't need to touch it.
          • A screen that's always on uses a lot of battery. Bring a power bank on long hikes.
          • Checking a text quickly is OK. Recording picks up again when you come back, but that stretch will be missing.

          Your coordinates (for 911 or Search and Rescue)

          Hold the phone flat, away from metal, magnets and cars. Points to magnetic north, like a real compass. If it seems wrong, move the phone in a figure-8 to recalibrate.
